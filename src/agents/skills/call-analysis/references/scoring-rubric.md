# Call Analysis Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to calibrate dimension ratings across calls.

## Rating scale

- `Strong`: clear positive signals with transcript evidence.
- `Moderate`: partial signals or mixed performance.
- `Weak`: clear deficiency or concerning pattern.
- `N/A`: no relevant data for this dimension in the call.

## Dimension calibration

### Rep Performance
- `Strong`: good talk-to-listen ratio (rep listens more than talks), asks discovery questions, handles objections with evidence, sets concrete next steps.
- `Moderate`: adequate questioning but missed opportunities, or dominated parts of the conversation, or next steps were vague.
- `Weak`: mostly monologue, no discovery questions, avoided or fumbled objections, no next steps set.

### Customer Signals
- `Strong`: explicit buying signals — verbal preference, urgency language, proactive questions about implementation or pricing.
- `Moderate`: engaged but non-committal — asking questions without urgency or positive sentiment without commitment.
- `Weak`: hesitation, pushback, disengagement, or competitor-preference signals.

### Key Decisions
- `Strong`: clear decisions made with stakeholder agreement — scope defined, timeline confirmed, or evaluation criteria locked.
- `Moderate`: directional agreement but details deferred ("we'll figure that out later").
- `Weak`: no decisions reached; conversation ended without resolution.

### Action Items
- `Strong`: specific actions with named owners and dates/deadlines documented.
- `Moderate`: actions discussed but owners or deadlines unclear.
- `Weak`: no action items, or vague "we'll follow up" without specifics.

### Risk Indicators
- `Strong` (low risk): no competitor mentions, no budget/timeline concerns, positive alignment signals.
- `Moderate`: some risk signals present — competitor mentioned, timeline questioned, or budget flagged but not blocking.
- `Weak` (high risk): competitor preferred, budget rejected, timeline pushed significantly, or key stakeholder disengaged.

### Momentum
- `Forward`: call advanced the deal — new commitments, deeper engagement, or clear next steps toward close.
- `Sideways`: call maintained status quo — no regression but no meaningful progress.
- `Backward`: call revealed setbacks — new objections, lost stakeholder support, or stalled commitments.

## Overall call verdict

- `Forward`: at least 3 dimensions Strong, no Weak on Momentum.
- `Sideways`: mixed profile or Moderate Momentum.
- `Backward`: Weak Momentum, or 2+ dimensions Weak.
