# Deal Health Check Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to calibrate ratings across deals.

## Rating scale

- `Green`: strong positive signals with concrete evidence.
- `Yellow`: mixed signals, gaps, or staleness that need attention.
- `Red`: clear warning signs or critical gaps.

## Dimension calibration

### Activity Recency
- `Green`: meaningful engagement within the last 7 days (meeting, email exchange, or channel activity).
- `Yellow`: 8-21 day gap since last meaningful interaction.
- `Red`: 21+ days silent — no meetings, emails, or messages.

### Stakeholder Coverage
- `Green`: Economic Buyer + Champion + Technical Evaluator all engaged and active.
- `Yellow`: some key roles identified but missing engagement or access.
- `Red`: only 1-2 contacts involved; no EB or Champion access.

### Next Steps Clarity
- `Green`: concrete actions with owners, dates, and clear deliverables documented.
- `Yellow`: vague "follow up" or "circle back" language without specifics.
- `Red`: no defined next step; deal drifting without direction.

### Competitive Position
- `Green`: preferred vendor signals — positive differentiation, verbal preference, or shortlist position.
- `Yellow`: active evaluation with competitors; no clear advantage.
- `Red`: losing signals — competitor preferred, feature gaps highlighted, or pricing pressure.

### Timeline Realism
- `Green`: close date aligns with current process stage and buyer-side signals.
- `Yellow`: tight but possible — requires acceleration or concurrent steps.
- `Red`: close date is unrealistic given stage, process gaps, or buyer silence.

## Overall health rating

- `Green`: at least 4 dimensions Green, and no Red.
- `Yellow`: mixed profile that does not meet Green or Red criteria.
- `Red`: 2 or more dimensions Red.

## Confidence guidance

- `High`: 4+ dimensions with direct evidence from the last 14 days.
- `Medium`: 3 dimensions with evidence; some signals stale or indirect.
- `Low`: sparse evidence, large gaps, or contradictions unresolved.
