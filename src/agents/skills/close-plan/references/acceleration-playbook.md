# Close Plan Acceleration Playbook

Use these tactics to close gaps faster without breaking the required approval sequence.

## Stakeholder gaps

- Schedule EB alignment call
- Elevate to executive sponsor

## Paper process delay

- Parallel-track legal and procurement
- Pre-send security documentation

## Stage drift

- Convert the next meeting into a decision meeting
- Introduce deadline pressure using an event-based close

## Guardrail

- Do not recommend acceleration that violates approval sequence.
