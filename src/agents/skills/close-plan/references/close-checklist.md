# Close Plan Close Checklist

Use this checklist to verify whether the deal has cleared the common gates needed to close.

## Common pre-close requirements

- Economic Buyer verbal alignment
- Champion confirmed
- Technical validation complete
- Security review approved
- Legal redlines resolved
- Procurement initiated
- Mutual action plan agreed

## Escalation rule

- If two or more items remain unchecked within 30 days of target close, escalate risk.
