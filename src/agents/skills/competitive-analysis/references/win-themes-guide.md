# Competitive Analysis Win Themes Guide

Use this guide to turn evidence into positioning angles without drifting into generic messaging.

## Win theme principles

- Tie differentiation to an observed deal risk.
- Anchor every claim in concrete evidence.
- Avoid feature dumping.
- Prioritize stakeholder-specific impact.

## Build each positioning angle with this pattern

1. Observed deal risk
2. Differentiating capability or structural advantage
3. Stakeholder impact
4. Proof signal

## Recommended phrasing pattern

"The buyer raised [risk]. Our [capability or advantage], supported by [proof], reduces [specific
stakeholder concern]."

## Good examples

- "Procurement is using implementation effort as leverage. Our documented faster deployment path
  reduces the buyer's perceived switching risk."
- "The champion asked for proof of enterprise readiness. Our named customer validation in the same
  segment addresses that concern more directly than generic feature claims."

## Anti-patterns

- "We are the leader in..."
- "Best-in-class..."
- Claims without transcript, email, or documented proof
- Messaging that ignores the stakeholder who actually raised the concern
