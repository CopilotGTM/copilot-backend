# MEDPIC Discovery Question Bank

Use these when the user asks for recommendations or when `Unknown` blocks scoring.

## Metrics

- "What quantified business outcome is most important to this project?"
- "How is success measured today, and what target should this change deliver?"
- "Who validated this ROI assumption on your side?"

## Economic Buyer

- "Who has final budget authority for this initiative?"
- "When can we include that person in the next decision checkpoint?"
- "Is approval single-owner or committee-based?"

## Decision Criteria

- "Which criteria will decide the shortlist: technical, commercial, and risk?"
- "How are those criteria weighted today?"
- "Which criteria are non-negotiable?"

## Paper Process

- "What approvals are required after business agreement (legal, procurement, security)?"
- "Who owns each approval stage?"
- "What is the realistic contract path and timing?"

## Identify Pain

- "What happens if this problem is not solved this quarter?"
- "Where is the impact visible today (revenue, cost, risk, productivity)?"
- "What deadline or trigger makes this urgent now?"

## Champion

- "Who is actively pushing this internally when we are not in the room?"
- "Can that person align other stakeholders and get EB time?"
- "What concrete action has this person taken recently to move the deal?"
