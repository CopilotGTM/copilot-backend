# MEDPIC Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to calibrate scores across deals.

## Rating scale

- `Strong`: current and explicit evidence supports the component.
- `Moderate`: partial evidence exists but has material gaps.
- `Weak`: evidence indicates the component is currently weak.
- `Unknown`: insufficient trustworthy evidence to score.

## Component calibration

### Metrics
- `Strong`: quantified outcome validated by buyer-side stakeholders.
- `Moderate`: directional outcome but weak quantification.
- `Weak`: value language only, no measurable business outcome.
- `Unknown`: no outcome evidence.

### Economic Buyer
- `Strong`: named authority with recent direct engagement.
- `Moderate`: likely EB identified, engagement is indirect or stale.
- `Weak`: authority unclear, conflicting signals, or no access path.
- `Unknown`: no reliable EB signal.

### Decision Criteria
- `Strong`: explicit criteria documented or clearly stated and stable.
- `Moderate`: criteria partly known or still evolving.
- `Weak`: criteria unclear, competitor-defined, or mostly speculative.
- `Unknown`: no decision criteria evidence.

### Paper Process
- `Strong`: procurement/legal/approval path and owners are mapped.
- `Moderate`: process partially known but key gates unclear.
- `Weak`: no clear path; repeated slips; unknown approvers.
- `Unknown`: no process evidence.

### Identify Pain
- `Strong`: pain is explicit, meaningful, and tied to impact or urgency.
- `Moderate`: pain is acknowledged but under-quantified or low urgency.
- `Weak`: weak motivation, "nice to have", or no compelling event.
- `Unknown`: no pain evidence.

### Champion
- `Strong`: active internal advocate with influence and access.
- `Moderate`: supportive contact with limited influence.
- `Weak`: no active advocate or only passive contact.
- `Unknown`: no champion evidence.

## Overall color rating

- `Green`: at least 4 `Strong`, and no more than 1 `Weak`/`Unknown`.
- `Yellow`: mixed profile that does not meet Green or Red.
- `Red`: 3 or more `Weak`/`Unknown`.

## Confidence guidance

- `High`: 5+ components with direct evidence in the last 30 days.
- `Medium`: 3-4 components with direct evidence; some indirect signals.
- `Low`: sparse or stale evidence, contradictions unresolved.
