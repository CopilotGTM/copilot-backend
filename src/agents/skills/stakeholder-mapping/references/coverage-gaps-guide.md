# Stakeholder Mapping Coverage Gaps Guide

Use this guide to identify structural stakeholder gaps and explain why they matter.

## Common gaps

- No identified EB
- Single-threading
- Champion without authority
- Technical evaluator missing in a technical evaluation
- Procurement or approval path unknown late in cycle
- Disengaged high-influence stakeholder
- Authority ambiguity

## Why gaps matter

- Increase late-stage slippage
- Lower forecast confidence
- Create silent veto exposure
- Hide approval-path risk

## Intervention framing

- Tie interventions to the specific structural gap observed.
- Avoid generic stakeholder expansion advice.
- Prioritize direct power-path clarification when authority is unclear.
