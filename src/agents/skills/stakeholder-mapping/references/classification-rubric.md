# Stakeholder Mapping Classification Rubric

Use this rubric to assign stakeholder roles and influence levels without over-inferring from titles.

## Role definitions

### Economic Buyer
- Evidence required: budget ownership language, final approval authority, or contract signatory
  signals.
- Title alone is insufficient.

### Technical Evaluator
- Evidence required: product requirement review, security review, compliance review, or technical
  evaluation ownership.

### End User
- Evidence required: usage discussion, workflow impact questions, or day-to-day adoption concerns.

### Coach
- Evidence required: internal advocacy language, strategy sharing, political guidance, or
  competitor insight.

### Blocker
- Evidence required: objection leadership, resistance signals, approval friction, or repeated
  negative gating behavior.

### Unknown
- Use when role evidence is incomplete, stale, or contradictory.

## Influence calibration

- `High`: controls budget, final decision, or the approval path.
- `Medium`: strong advisory, evaluation, or gatekeeping role.
- `Low`: limited, local, or informational influence only.
- `Unknown`: insufficient evidence.

## Tie-break rules

- Prefer explicit decision language over inferred title.
- Prefer recent first-party evidence over older summaries.
- If credible sources conflict and cannot be reconciled, use `Unknown`.
